Methodology & limitations

Transparent decision support, not a formal audit.

The assessments are designed to structure an executive conversation and identify relative intervention priorities. They do not replace production evidence, financial validation, regulatory assessment or independent assurance.

Scoring direction

Event, incident and change scores are intervention scores: higher means greater concern. Resilience is a readiness score: higher means stronger capability. The dashboard keeps these measures visibly separate.

Illustrative defaults

Pre-populated values are labelled as an example scenario. Replace them with a representative month, quarter or annual average before interpreting the output.

Financial assumptions

Capacity value, incident exposure and change exposure are displayed separately because they may overlap. Do not add them without validating scope, causality and exclusions with Finance and Risk.

Evidence standard

Validate outputs using monitoring data, incident and change records, service maps, exercise results, business-owner sign-off and current third-party assurance.

Interpretation bands

0–34 controlled, 35–54 targeted improvement, 55–74 high-priority intervention, and 75–100 immediate executive priority. Bands indicate relative attention, not regulatory compliance.

Confidence and limitations

Outputs are only as reliable as the input definitions and source data. Document assumptions, time period, missing evidence and confidence before using results for investment decisions.
