Executive case studies

Evidence of measurable operational impact.

Three examples of how I diagnose operational problems, create leadership alignment and improve service outcomes.

Case study 01

Reduced failed changes by 60% year on year

Business problem

Repeated post-release instability reduced confidence in production change.

Leadership insight

The issue was not change volume alone, but inconsistent readiness, validation and ownership.

Strengthened service acceptance, release controls and post-change accountability—improving stability and engineering confidence.
Case study 02

Reduced weekly monitoring events from ~3,500 to ~1,500

Business problem

Operational noise consumed engineering capacity and obscured meaningful service signals.

Leadership insight

Alert volume was a capacity and response-quality issue, not merely a tooling issue.

Prioritised alert rationalisation and observability improvements—recovering engineering attention for reliability work.
Case study 03

Business problem

A complex enterprise milestone carried significant customer, operational and partner risk.

Leadership insight

Success depended on end-to-end readiness, command governance and recovery preparation.

Aligned teams around service continuity and recovery readiness—delivering near-zero customer disruption.
